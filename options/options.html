hh Fit Scorer

Scores how well you fit a vacancy while you browse hh.ru. Your key and profile never leave this browser except in the scoring call itself.

Your Jev API key

Stored only in chrome.storage.local. Never committed, never sent anywhere except api.typesafe.ai.

Your profile

This is who you are. Scoring stays disabled until it is filled — an empty profile produces confident nonsense rather than an obvious error.

Availability

Internship and junior postings test these constantly — "30–40 часов в неделю", "4 курс бакалавриата". Without them the model has nothing to compare against and returns a meaningless mid-range guess.

Quick start — paste your resume instead

If you fill this with at least ~120 characters, it alone is enough to enable scoring. The structured fields above give better results.

Preferences

This is what you want, kept separate from who you are. These drive the dealbreaker checks that cap the score.

Weights

How much each component counts toward the overall score. Renormalised automatically when a component is unavailable, so they need not sum to 1.

Requirement checklist

Lines are sorted into kinds (requirement, nice-to-have, eligibility, heading, duty, other) by the model, so there is no "is it a requirement" threshold to tune. Only hard requirements count toward the score; items your profile doesn't mention are excluded rather than failed.
